# Gravitational Imaging Teaching Team

Gravitational lensing starts with a simple idea: mass changes the path that light takes. Our workshop connects that idea to optical lenses, dark matter, strong-lens images, and space telescopes, with demonstrations that let participants experiment before we ask them to interpret the sky.

The curriculum is still taking shape. The current outline moves through four parts, from dark matter and optics to strong lenses and space telescopes, with hands-on activities along the way.
